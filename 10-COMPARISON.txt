This example exaggerates to make a point:

❌ WHAT NOT TO DO:
  • Provide terrible service to regular customers
  • Optimize costs at expense of genuine helpfulness
  • Create massive quality gaps between tiers
  • Make customers feel undervalued

✅ WHAT TO DO INSTEAD:
  • Use smart routing for genuine complexity differences
  • Maintain quality standards across all tiers
  • Be transparent about service levels
  • Ensure "basic" tier is still genuinely helpful
  • Route based on query complexity, not customer "value"

THE MESSAGE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━
While this example shows extreme differences for comedic effect, it reflects
real tensions in AI deployment. The goal is to learn the TECHNICAL PATTERNS
while rejecting the ETHICAL APPROACH shown here.

Good AI support treats all users with respect while still optimizing costs
intelligently based on actual query complexity, not perceived customer value.
